How to play

  1. Get your group in. One person creates a room, then shares the five-character code. Play with 2–8 people.
  2. Take your turn. Answer the trivia question and choose a neutral or rival territory.
  3. Claim the ground. Correct answers capture that territory, even if an opponent owns it. Misses still pass the turn.
  4. Win the map. Play three rounds. Most territories wins; ties go to the first name alphabetically.

No login needed. Your room, turn, map and score are shared live across every device.
